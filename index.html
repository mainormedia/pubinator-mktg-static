06·AI copyedit

AI that asks first.

Run a copyedit pass and get tracked changes, not a rewrite. Each suggestion waits for an editor to accept or reject it.

  • Your style guideAP style plus your house rules.
  • Every change visibleDeletions struck through, additions underlined, a reason for each.
  • Editors decideNothing is applied until a person accepts it.

Copyedit · AP + house style · 3 suggestions

The library board voted 5-2 on Tuesday to cut Sunday hours at the Eastside branch starting Jan. 4th.

Board president Ruth Okafor said the change saves roughly $60,000.00$60,000 a year. Residents can comment at the board's Nov. 12 meeting.

1 of 3 · AP style

Remove "on" before a day of the week.

2 of 3 · AP style

Use "Jan. 4," not "Jan. 4th," for dates.

3 of 3 · House style

Drop ".00" from whole-dollar amounts.

12·FAQ

Questions from the desk.

Don't see yours? Write to us at [hello@pubinator.com].

Does Pubinator replace InDesign or our layout tools?

No. Designers keep laying out pages where they do now. Pubinator tracks the work around the layout: stories, photos, ads, versions, review rounds and the final files that go to press.

What works today, and what's coming?

Print production works today: issues, sections, articles, ads, contributors and final files. Web, Newsletter, Podcast and Video channels are in development and are marked Coming on this page.

Do contributors need an account?

No. Send a freelancer an upload link tied to a file request; it closes on the date you set. Staff get accounts with roles.

Does the AI change our copy?

Only when an editor accepts a change. Copyedits come back as tracked changes, one at a time, each with Accept and Reject.

Can we bring our back issues?

Yes. The Archive plan puts past issues online, and founding publishers get help with the import.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the plan and the number of titles. Book a demo and we'll walk through it. Founding publishers keep a founding rate.

Who makes Pubinator?

Pubinator is made by Mainor Media LLC, a design and publishing production studio.
